\problemname{minimum}

\section{Problem}
Given two integers N and M, find the minimum of the two.

\section{Input}
The input consists of two integers N and M separated by white-space where $0 \leq N \leq 1000$ and $0 \leq M \leq 1000$.


\section{Output}
The output should be a single integer, the minimum of N and M.
